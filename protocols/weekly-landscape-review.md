# Weekly landscape review protocol

## Purpose

Convert a rolling seven-day evidence window into a governed comparison against the prior four-week baseline. The review must detect actor behavior, pattern movement, contradictions, opportunity pressure, and repository implications without mistaking novelty for importance.

## Actor lenses

Review providers, suppliers, builders, buyers, participants, media, funders, analysts, regulators, and researchers separately. Record observable behavior before interpreting motive. A claim such as “buyers are shifting” is invalid unless tied to transactions, requests, budgets, procurement language, adoption, churn, waitlists, pilot activity, or another inspectable behavior.

## Operating sequence

Collection captures source-backed events. Verification separates fact from inference. Normalization maps events into actors, observations, and relationships. Baseline comparison classifies each pattern as new, strengthening, stable, weakening, contradicted, or expired. Socratic descent identifies the underlying problem, beneficiary, mechanism, dangerous assumption, and falsifier. Analogue review compares one direct example, one adjacent example, and one failed or constrained example. Strategy synthesis proposes no more than three strategies. Experiment design proposes no more than two bounded tests. Genesis review may surface no more than one candidate and must be able to return none.

## Required weekly outputs

The human review explains what changed, why it matters, who is moving, what remains uncertain, what not to transfer, and what action is justified. The machine record lists observations, relationships, pattern deltas, contradictions, opportunity dispositions, experiments, repository implications, collection failures, and no-change receipts.

## Materiality

An event is material when it adds a primary source, changes confidence by at least 0.15, opens or closes an opportunity, alters policy or funding conditions, demonstrates adoption or failure, changes repository risk, or creates a new cross-domain mechanism. Repetition, commentary without new evidence, and cosmetic announcements are not material.

## Review boundary

The weekly review may recommend, classify, and prepare dossiers. It may not mark knowledge accepted, create a production repository, merge code, contact external parties, submit applications, spend money, or make public claims without explicit approval.
