# Full vision and phased horizons

## Horizon 0 — Foundation

Repository-native JSON records, validation, deterministic indexes, relationship integrity, provenance receipts, protocols, notebook boundaries, and daily automation-compatible run output.

## Horizon 1 — Operational intelligence

A reliable daily intake produces source-backed observations and a weekly review compares behavior and pattern movement against a rolling baseline. Human approval remains mandatory for accepted knowledge and repository actions.

## Horizon 2 — Strategy engine

The graph can assemble competing strategic interpretations, expose counterevidence, trace repository implications, and recommend bounded experiments with measurable kill criteria.

## Horizon 3 — Genesis engine

High-confidence opportunities can produce complete genesis dossiers, but repository creation remains a governed human decision. The engine proves why the concept requires a new boundary and why the smallest test cannot be contained elsewhere.

## Horizon 4 — Ecosystem portfolio intelligence

The system models shared primitives, duplicated effort, maintenance burden, licensing, funding pathways, collaborator roles, and cross-repository dependencies. It can advise when to merge, split, archive, spin out, or federate work.

## Horizon 5 — Longshot research organism

External research, repository evidence, experiments, economic signals, ecological effects, and community outcomes form a regenerative learning loop. The system can recognize category formation early, preserve paradox and contradiction, and recommend new institutional or technical forms without claiming certainty it has not earned.

The longshot horizon does not relax the gates. Greater scope requires stronger provenance, stronger disconfirmation, and greater human accountability.
