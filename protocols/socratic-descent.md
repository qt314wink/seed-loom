# Socratic descent protocol

Socratic descent prevents the system from treating a fashionable surface signal as a proven opportunity.

Begin with the observed event and ask what literally changed. Ask who changed behavior, what cost or constraint motivated it, and what evidence would exist if the interpretation were true. Descend from event to behavior, from behavior to need, from need to mechanism, from mechanism to intervention, and from intervention to the smallest falsifiable experiment.

For every serious opportunity answer: What is being claimed? Which part is verified? What is inferred? Who experiences the problem? Who pays, supplies, builds, regulates, funds, covers, or analyzes it? What changed now? What would happen without intervention? What existing substitute is being used? Why is that substitute inadequate? Which assumption carries the most risk? What observation would disprove the thesis? What evidence is conspicuously absent? What simpler explanation fits the same facts? What should not be concluded yet?

The descent stops when the next question cannot be answered from evidence. The result is then classified as watch, reject, or experiment rather than promoted through rhetoric.
