# Implementation boundaries

The knowledge graph is repository-native in v0. JSON files are canonical; generated indexes and graph views are disposable projections. A graph database may be added later only when query scale, collaboration, or visualization requirements prove the need.

Collection systems may create captured, candidate, and source-backed observations. They may not create accepted knowledge. Strategy systems may propose dispositions and experiments. They may not merge code, create production repositories, spend funds, submit grants, or contact participants. Repository tooling may prepare branches and draft artifacts only within explicitly allowed paths.

Facts, inferences, significance, confidence, assumptions, limitations, and counterevidence remain distinct fields. Contradictions are preserved as relationships. Superseded records remain addressable. Generated output must never overwrite hand-reviewed records without a content hash and explicit approval.

Notebooks are exploratory surfaces. Durable claims graduate from notebooks only through schema validation, provenance checks, and review gates. Visualizations are views, not sources of truth.
