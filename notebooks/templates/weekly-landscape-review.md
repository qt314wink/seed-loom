# Weekly landscape review notebook template

## Retrieval window

Record the current seven-day window, the prior four-week baseline, source coverage, missing providers, and collection failures.

## Actor behavior map

For providers, suppliers, builders, buyers, participants, media, funders, analysts, regulators, and researchers, record observable actions, evidence references, confidence, and the change relative to baseline.

## Pattern movement

For each candidate pattern, record whether it is new, strengthening, stable, weakening, contradicted, or expired. Preserve alternative explanations.

## Socratic descent

State the surface signal, verified facts, inferred need, beneficiary, mechanism, existing substitute, dangerous assumption, falsifier, absent evidence, and simpler explanation.

## Analogue reconstruction

Compare one direct analogue, one adjacent analogue, and one failed or constrained analogue. Extract repeatable mechanisms and non-transferable context.

## Disposition

Choose reject, watch, experiment, existing-repo, cross-repo, or genesis-candidate. Explain the smallest justified next action and the approval gate.
